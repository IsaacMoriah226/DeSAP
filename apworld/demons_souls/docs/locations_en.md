# Demon's Souls Remake: locations

Every location is named `<area code> <world number>: <original item> - <where>`, for example
`GB 1-1: Mail Breaker - first fog, corridor end`: the code and number of the area the item is in, the item it holds
in the unrandomized game, and a short description of the spot. Descriptions use made-up names for parts of each
area; this guide defines them, area by area, and lists every location with what it needs.

## Getting into each part

1-1 is open from the start. Every other part needs its item (`1-2 The Lord's Path`, ..., `5-3 Rotting Haven`),
and also what the vanilla game needs to open its archstone:

- 2-1, 3-1, 4-1 and 5-1: Phalanx dead (the Monumental unseals the other archstones after the first boss).
- x-2 and x-3 (and 1-4): the previous part's boss dead (its archstone appears when you take its soul).
- 1-3 and 1-4: also the archdemon of another world dead and its soul taken (Dragon God, Old Monk, Storm King or
  Maiden Astraea): the gate past the Tower Knight stays sealed until then.

The client keeps you out of a part you cannot enter yet: it cannot be warped to, and walking into it sends you back
to the Nexus. The goal is the credits: kill all five archdemons (Old King Allant, Dragon God, Old Monk, Storm King,
Maiden Astraea), talk to the Maiden in Black and defeat False King Allant below the Nexus.

## Missable locations

Locations marked *missable* can be lost or only exist under conditions the logic cannot see (a world tendency,
an NPC who must stay alive or be helped, the tutorial, which cannot be revisited). They only ever hold filler, so
missing them never costs you or anyone else anything important.

## NX: The Nexus

Terms used in this area's names:

- **ground floor**: The Nexus's main floor where you wake up: the five archstones, Boldwin, Stockpile Thomas, the Maiden in Black and the central seal.
- **curved stairs**: The two curved staircases that climb from the ground floor around the central chamber up to the door of the Monumental's level.
- **ledges**: Two broken ledges partway up the curved stairs: one directly above Blacksmith Boldwin, its mirror on the other side. Reached by dropping onto them from the curved stairs above.
- **outer stairs**: The staircase outside the central chamber (not the one with the archstones), visible from near Boldwin, that leads up to the second floor.
- **second floor**: The gallery at the top of the outer stairs, at the back of the Nexus, where Yurt waits after his rescue and where Mephistopheles appears.
- **Monumental's balcony**: The top level, behind the door that opens after your first boss kill; the Monumental sits there and a narrow staircase leads down from the balcony.
- **Pantheon**: The hall of fame behind the door at the very top of the Nexus; the game only opens it in online mode.
- **below the Nexus**: The end-game area under the central seal (same map, about 1000 m lower): the forest of branches, the fog door and False King Allant's arena inside the Old One.

## TUT: Tutorial (Boletarian Palace)

Terms used in this area's names:

- **Outpost Passage**: First tutorial map (m08_00): the flooded tunnel where the game starts, up to the swamp and the archstone that takes you into the fort.
- **stair landing**: The candlelit landing in the corridor after the long staircase, where the first Dregling lunges at you.
- **broken bridge**: The broken wooden bridge ending at wooden railings that you vault over into the swamp with four Dreglings.
- **Forlorn Outpost**: Second tutorial map (m08_01): the fort with Boletarian Soldiers and Blue Eye Knights, ending at the Vanguard's arena.
- **crates**: The hall at the top of the wooden stairs, filled with boxes and barrels.
- **crossbowman's archway**: The next room, where a crossbow soldier stands under an archway on the left.
- **fog door**: The fog (light wall) after the furnace-room stairs; the path to the Vanguard continues straight, a side room is to the right.
- **boss**: The Vanguard (tutorial boss).
- **Unknown Egress**: Third tutorial map (m08_02), only reached by beating the Vanguard and touching his archstone: a short torch-lit hall that ends at the Dragon God.
- **upper hall**: The hallway at the arrival level, ending at a staircase.
- **foot of stairs**: The bottom of the staircase on the left that leads to the lower hall.
- **first torch**: The first torch on the right of the lower hall.
- **glowing remains**: The row of five glowing corpses down the lower hall (two shards, then a side-by-side pair and a last one); just past them walking toward the Dragon God ends the tutorial.

## GB 1-1: Gates of Boletaria

Terms used in this area's names:

- **start**: The archstone arrival point and the bonfire-lit stone bridge and stairs leading up toward the main gate. 'Behind palisade' is the wooden palisade behind you at the arrival point.
- **locked door**: The door on the left of the starting bridge that leads down into the execution grounds. It opens only at pure white or pure black world tendency and stays open once you have gone through it.
- **execution grounds**: The sunken yard behind the locked door, below the starting bridge: a pile of corpses guarded by Black Phantom Dreglings (and a Crystal Lizard), a slope up past an execution platform, and the stairwell building where Executioner Miralda hides (pure white only). 'Pile 1-5' are the five corpses of the corpse pile, in game-file order; 'upper slope' is the body guarded by five more Black Phantom Dreglings.
- **stairwell**: The stairwell building of the execution grounds (Miralda's hiding place); its stairs lead down to the ladder into the corpse pit.
- **corpse pit**: The deep corpse-disposal pit below the execution grounds, reached by a ladder from the stairwell building. 'Beam' is one of the wooden beams spanning it halfway down; 'bottom' is the floor of the pit.
- **courtyard**: The open yard in front of the main (boss) gate, between the left and right towers. The two hanging corpses fall here once their chains are broken.
- **hanging corpse**: The two corpses hung by chains from the left tower's stairway balcony; break both chains and they fall into the courtyard.
- **wooden scaffold**: The wooden structure with a crossbow soldier, up the small staircase left of the courtyard, before the small door into the west rooms.
- **west rooms**: The rooms behind the small door left of the courtyard, leading to the first fog door (Firebomb crates in the room with a staircase).
- **first fog**: The first fog door, at the end of the west rooms. Right after it a corridor leads right to the barrels and the Mail Breaker.
- **boulder stairs**: The long stairway past the firebomb soldiers where a soldier rolls a boulder down. 'Corridor end' is the dead end you reach by turning back before climbing them; 'below' is the lower floor beside them (behind the barricade the boulder breaks, or by dropping through the gap).
- **catapults**: The walkway with siege machines at the top of the boulder stairs; turning left leads to the Red Eye Knight and the Mausoleum.
- **mausoleum**: Old King Doran's building at the end of the catapult walkway, guarded by a Red Eye Knight; its door needs the Mausoleum Key. The altar inside holds Demonbrandt.
- **second fog**: The fog door beside the Blue Eye Knight at the top of the boulder stairs (opposite side from the catapults). 'Ledge below second fog' is the lower terrace you drop to through the gap on the right of it.
- **left tower**: The tower on the left of the courtyard. Its top room (past the Blue Eye Knight, without taking the second fog) has the Firebomb corpse; its stairs pass the balcony with the two chains and end at a lever that opens the side gate into the courtyard.
- **Ostrava's ledge**: The railing ledge past the second fog, where Ostrava stands above the Dreglings.
- **Ostrava's pit**: The room below Ostrava's ledge with six Dreglings and explosive barrels.
- **building past Ostrava**: The building straight ahead after Ostrava's pit, before following Ostrava to the right; a corridor with the Scimitar at its end.
- **Ostrava's route**: The path Ostrava walks after his rescue, ending in a little corner guarded by a melee and a ranged soldier.
- **barrel room**: The room below the little staircase near Ostrava's route where eight Dreglings hide among explosive barrels.
- **long corridor**: The long corridor inside the building you enter from Ostrava's pit; its left end has a corpse, a soldier guards the gate at its other end.
- **wooden ledge**: The wooden ledge with a hanging body by the crossbow soldier at the top of the stairs past the barrel room.
- **third fog**: The fog door at the top of the stone staircase with a Blue Eye Knight; it leads to the boulder cage, the crossroads and the dragon areas.
- **dragon bridge**: The bridge, full of soldiers, that the dragon burns, crossing above the boss arena from the crossroads to the lever room. 'Dragon bridge end, stairs' is the little staircase down to the right at its far end.
- **lever room**: The room at the far end of the dragon bridge with the lever that opens the main gate. 'Past lever room' is the corridor beyond it with breakable furniture.
- **right tower**: The tower on the right of the courtyard, reached from the lever room side; Hoplites on its bridge and stairs. 'Balcony' is through the hole in its wall on the way down; at the bottom a lever opens its door into the courtyard.
- **dragon roost**: The open field where the two dragons nest, reached by turning right at the crossroads before the dragon bridge and going down the stairs past the columned hall. 'Entrance' is the corpse nearest the stairs; 'twin corpses 1/2' are the two Renowned Warrior Soul corpses side by side; 'lower ledge' is the lower ledge below the roost where two corpses lie together.
- **execution grounds stairwell**: The stairwell building of the execution grounds, where Executioner Miralda hides (pure white tendency only).

- **archstone ledge**: Where you arrive from the Nexus: up the stairs on the left are the Filthy Man, the bottom of Ed's elevator shaft behind him, and a Crystal Lizard; the ledge continues past the lizard to a broken cart.

- **rotating elevator**: The continuously moving lift reached through the boulder building. It stops at the balcony room (up) and at its bottom stop (down).
- **balcony room**: The room at the rotating elevator's upper stop with a Fat Official and two miners; a hanging corpse is on its balcony.
- **rotating elevator bottom**: The outdoor floor at the rotating elevator's lowest stop, below the boulder building; the Primeval Demon appears here in black tendency.
- **miners' path**: The long lower tunnels with two miner groups and the pottery room where dogs burst out of jars, leading to the ore room.
- **ore room**: The Fat Official's room at the end of the miners' path, with two corpses (left and right).
- **side tunnel**: The tunnel up the stairs past the ambush doorway left of the pottery room (wiki 'Point C'): Crescent Moon Grass at its end, Pickaxe in the side room off it.
- **crumbling bridge**: The wooden platform above the side tunnel and the bridge that collapses toward the Fat Official's platform on the far side.
- **Fat Official platform**: The platform at the far end of the crumbling bridge where a Fat Official casts fire.
- **lever gallery**: The corridors below the crumbling bridge (where you land after jumping) with miner groups and the gate lever.
- **first fog**: The first fog gate, beyond the door opened by the gate lever; leads into the upper mine.
- **cog ramps**: Just past the first fog: the big rotating wooden cog and the wooden ramps going up.

- **cliff**: The outdoor ledge above the archstone past the four miners, at the end of the corridor after the fire lizard room.

- **second fog**: The fog gate on the left of the corridor with three miners, between the fire lizard room and the cliff.

- **horde hall**: The long hall left of the workshop with the miner horde and two rock throwers; a cart at its end hides the Steel Shield.

- **lizard tower**: The wooden tower topped by four Fire Lizards; you descend it past a ramp with three dogs to the room with five miners and the magma wheel.
- **magma wheel**: The wheel at the bottom of the lizard tower; turning it plays a cutscene that cools the magma pit.
- **magma pit**: The pit below the horde hall that is molten (damage floor) until the magma wheel is turned; a hole in a grille leads to a side corpse.
- **barrel room**: The room of explosive barrels with a Fat Official, up the stairs from the cooled magma pit; a fog gate is behind him.

- **boss arena**: The Armor Spider's long tunnel beyond the last fog gate; the archstone appears behind the boss.
- **cliff corridor**: The corridor with four miners just before the cliff above the archstone.
- **magma wheel room**: The room with five miners and the magma wheel at the bottom of the lizard tower.

## TC 2-2: The Tunnel City

Terms used in this area's names:

- **elevator hall**: Left branch from the Armor Spider archstone: the wide room with the giant elevator in the middle and a Fat Official on the right.
- **cart room**: The room with many mine carts and rock worms at the end of the narrow corridor after the elevator hall.
- **rift platforms**: The wooden platforms you drop through one by one below the cart room, ending in a corridor (Sharpstone Chunk) and stairs down to the bearbug ledge.
- **bearbug ledge**: The ledge at the bottom of those stairs where Patches sets his trap: the Club is the bait, a Giant Bearbug drops in, and the Flame Resistance Ring glows beside where it lands.
- **bearbug den**: Left of the bearbug ledge: four small bearbugs, stairs to the Great Axe, and a Giant Bearbug guarding a corpse.
- **side room off bearbug den**: The room behind a door along the wall below the bearbug den, with a miner and two corpses.
- **lizard nest**: The Crystal Lizard nest reached by following the two lizards from the lower elevator room (the elevator goes back up to the elevator hall); two Black Phantoms guard it.
- **rift path**: The outdoor path that descends from the bearbug area along the rift, with flying bearbugs, three rock worms at a junction, ore carts and the Filthy Man near its bottom.
- **bug tunnels**: The underground tunnel system entered by a fog gate at the bottom of the rift path; it splits three ways near the lava rift and has several side tunnels with breakable bones and spines. It is the long route to the pit bottom.
- **worm cliff**: The open area in the bug tunnels with ore carts, three rock worms and flying bearbugs coming from the edge.
- **lava rift**: The molten area off the bug tunnels full of Giant Bearbugs; the lava is a damage floor.
- **great pit**: Right branch from the archstone: past two miner groups, the huge pit with wooden platforms you drop down (Plague Resistance Ring, a corpse hanging on a narrow beam, Scirvir's cave with two Crystal Lizards, a lizard walkway and stairs near the bottom). It is the short route.
- **pit bottom**: The floor of the great pit where the short and long routes meet; rock worms, ladders back up.
- **worm cave**: The bigger room through the tunnel from the pit bottom, guarded by three spitting rock worms.
- **boss approach**: The path from the pit bottom to the Flamelurker fog gate.
- **boss arena**: Flamelurker's room (bones, columns and stairs) where the archstone appears.
- **Scirvir's cave**: The cave halfway down the great pit with two Crystal Lizards, where Scirvir stands.

## PH 3-1: Prison of Hope

Terms used in this area's names:

- **3F west**: The starting floor's first cell block: the corridor outside the starting cell. In-game floor plates call the block of the first corridor on every floor 'West' and the second one 'East'.
- **3F east**: The second corridor of the starting floor, reached through the open aisle from 3F West; it ends at the 3F Key on the wall.
- **stair end**: The end of each corridor with the stairwells and big iron doors (the 3F Key door, the 4F/2F/1F entrances), on the side of the starting cell's first right turn.
- **plate end**: The opposite end of each corridor, where the floor-name plates hang and where the Mind Flayer halls and wall keys are.
- **outer row**: Cells along the outer wall of a corridor (the starting cell is one).
- **inner row**: Cells between the West and East corridors of a floor, back to back.
- **4F west**: The first cell block upstairs, behind the 3F Key door and the stairs; it has the iron maiden cells and the hole in the wall.
- **4F east**: The 4F corridor with the Once Royal Mistress, reached through the 4F Key gate and the balcony; it ends at the iron gate down to Freke's block.
- **hole cell**: The 4F West inner-row cell with two iron maidens (Black Leather set) and a hole in its back wall.
- **4F east, dead end**: The short dead end through the hole in the hole cell, past the poison iron maiden, where the 4F Key and 4F Cell Key hang.
- **big locked cell**: The large 4F West cell at the plate end, behind a 4F Cell Key door, with jar prisoners (Secret Dagger, Claws).
- **4F balcony**: The outside walkway between the 4F Key gate and the gate into 4F East.
- **4F east iron maidens**: The 4F East cell with five iron maidens (four back to back, one poison trap), behind a 4F Cell Key door.
- **Freke's block**: The sealed end of 3F East reached by the stairs down from 4F East: an entry room and the cell row with Sage Freke.
- **jailer's hall**: The mezzanine hall between 3F and 2F at the foot of the stairs from Freke's block, with the 1F Key and 1F Cell Key on the wall and two Warden's Key gates.
- **jailer's corridor**: The two corridors behind the Warden's Key gates of the jailer's hall (one over each 2F block); they meet at a big iron gate toward the ballista side.
- **2F west**: Lord Rydell's block, entered through the fog door at the bottom of the jailer's hall stairs. Rydell is in the outer-row cell at the plate end.
- **2F west inner row**: The 2F West cells opposite Rydell's row. They are behind the rubble that only clears in Pure White (Three-Cornered Hat, Venerable Sage set).
- **2F east**: The second corridor of 2F, reached by stairs up from 1F near the door out to the ballista bridge; its gate only opens after Rydell has been freed and talked to.
- **Rydell's corpse**: The corpse that appears in an open 2F East inner-row cell once Rydell has left 2F West.
- **1F west**: The bottom floor's first corridor, behind the 1F Key door at the bottom of the stairs.
- **1F east**: The bottom floor's second corridor (Crystal Lizard); its plate end has a standing coffin hiding a door outside.
- **horde hall**: The hall at the stair end of 1F between the two corridors, with the Prisoner Horde and six jars.
- **outer stairs**: The outside walkway and stairs past the coffin-hidden door at the end of 1F East (Estoc, Moonlightstone Shard).
- **ballista bridge**: The long walkway from the 1F stair-end door to the arrow-shooting statue (ballista), with narrow rails below its railing and many dead bodies on it.
- **north church balcony**: The upper balcony on the Silver Catalyst side of the church before the boss, reached by the spiral staircase.
- **Liar's room**: The room at the end of the opposite (south) church balcony, where the Liar keeps the boss alive; the Special Key hangs on its wall.
- **catwalk ledge**: The small ledge halfway down the raised catwalk under the Liar's balcony.
- **boss arena**: The Fool's Idol church hall behind the fog door.
- **below catwalk ledge**: The ground below the catwalk ledge, under the Liar's balcony, near the church before the boss (a crossbow black phantom hides there).

## RH 5-3: Rotting Haven

Terms used in this area's names:

- **upper descent**: Holes and ladders leading down from the Dirty Colossus archstone to the boss fog gate.
- **cavern**: The boss arena past the boss fog gate at the bottom of the upper descent (the fight starts with a cutscene on entry): a vast cavern with Depraved Ones praying to the Maiden.
- **plague swamp**: Blood/plague pool at the bottom of the cavern full of plague babies, below the entrance cliffs; Maiden Astraea is at its far (east) end behind Garl Vinland.
- **pier**: Small wooden pier in the plague swamp.
- **plague swamp end**: The far end of the plague swamp, where Garl Vinland guards Maiden Astraea.
