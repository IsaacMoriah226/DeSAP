# Demon's Souls Remake

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every item pickup (corpses, chests), every boss reward and scripted reward, the fixed drops of one-time enemies
(minibosses, black phantoms, the Fat Officials, ...) and the limited shop entries become Archipelago locations:
what you find there may be an item from your world or from someone else's.

Each world part (1-2, 1-3, ... 5-3) is locked behind an Archipelago item named after it (`1-2 The Lord's Path`,
..., `5-3 Rotting Haven`); 1-1 is open from the start. The game's own conditions stay on top: x-n also needs the
boss of x-(n-1) dead, the other worlds open after Phalanx, and 1-3 needs an archdemon of another world dead. The
client enforces the locks: walking into a part you cannot enter yet sends you back to the Nexus.

Options can randomize each class's starting gear, remove stat requirements, upgrade received weapons to match
yours, and turn on DeathLink.

## What is the goal?

Slay the five archdemons and False King Allant below the Nexus.

## What does another world's item look like?

A Nexial Binding, named after the item on nearby corpses and chests ("Friend's Moonlight Greatsword"),
"Archipelago Item" for boss and scripted rewards, "[AP] Item for another world" in shops.

## Which locations are missable?

These only ever hold filler (118 of 656):
- the tutorial (it cannot be revisited);
- items that need a world tendency (pure white or pure black), and tendency-only enemies;
- items behind NPCs who can die or turn hostile, or who must be rescued first (Biorr, Rydell, Blige, Patches, Yurt,
  the Mausoleum Key from Ostrava, ...);
- enemy drops that are lost if not picked up before the area reloads;
- the items that appear as Depraved Ones fall into the 5-1 chasm;
- Soulbrandt (after the final boss) and the shops.
