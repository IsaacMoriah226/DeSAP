# Demon's Souls Remake

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every item pickup (corpses, chests), every boss reward and a few shop entries become Archipelago locations: what
you find there may be an item from your world or from someone else's. Each world part (1-2, 1-3, ... 5-3) is
locked behind an Archipelago item named after it (`1-2 The Lord's Path`, ..., `5-3 Rotting Haven`); 1-1 is open
from the start. The parts of a world are sequential: x-n needs both its own item and the boss of x-(n-1) dead,
even when you already hold the x-n item.

The client enforces the locks: a part you cannot enter yet cannot be warped to from the Nexus, and walking into
it sends you back to the Nexus.

## What is the goal?

Reach the ending credits: slay the archdemons and the Old One.

## Which locations are missable?

Items that only appear at a world tendency (pure white or pure black, or a partial tendency), and one-time events
such as the tutorial's Vanguard fight. These locations only ever hold filler.
