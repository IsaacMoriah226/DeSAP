# First Playthrough — What to Look For

Thanks for testing. Set up with `PLAYER_GUIDE.md`, play normally, and note anything below that goes wrong or feels
off. Screenshots help. When reporting, send the newest `C:\ProgramData\Archipelago\logs\DemonsSoulsLauncher_*.txt`
and say roughly when it happened.

Already checked by us: connecting, the Ready! pause, treasure pickups (yours and other players'), received items,
world locks, the tutorial, starting gear, the overlay, the map tracker. The rest is new ground.

## Setup

- [ ] Installing the `.apworld` and finding **Demon's Souls Launcher** in the Archipelago Launcher.
- [ ] Choosing `KytyLauncher.exe`; the game starts with your usual settings.
- [ ] "Preparing Archipelago" then "Ready!" at the title. How long did it take?

## Things nobody has tested yet

- [ ] **Boss souls.** Each boss's Demon Soul is a location: after the kill you get whatever was placed there.
- [ ] **Scripted rewards.** NPC gifts and event items (for example Demonbrandt in the 1-1 mausoleum).
- [ ] **One-time enemy drops.** Minibosses and NPC phantoms that drop a fixed item once (e.g. the 1-1 execution grounds
  stairwell, the Fat Officials' armor, the Colorless Demon Souls).
- [ ] **Shops.** Entries that hold Archipelago items show as "[AP] ...". Buying one sends it.
- [ ] **DeathLink** (if someone else has it on): dying kills them, their death kills you. Not in the tutorial.
- [ ] **Goal.** Killing False King Allant finishes your game ("Goal complete" in the overlay).
- [ ] **Corpses with several items for others** show one token per item, each named after it.
- [ ] **Overlay text** is the Jost font (geometric, Futura-like), not a default font.
- [ ] **Map tracker:** does "in logic" match what you can actually reach? Is your position right?

## Also worth noting

- Anything that crashed the game or the launcher.
- Items you didn't get, or got twice.
- Being sent back to the Nexus when you shouldn't be (or not when you should).
- Anything confusing in the launcher, the overlay or the tracker.
